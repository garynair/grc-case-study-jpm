# Task 9: Conduct the Incident Tabletop and Present to Executives

**Suggested time:** 120 minutes · **Primary lead:** Incident Lead with all students participating

> **Required outputs**
>
> Tabletop decision log; communication matrix; one-page executive report; five-minute presentation.

## Why this task matters

The final task combines governance, risk, controls, incident response and communication in a realistic management exercise.

## Learning objectives

- Make time-sensitive decisions using incomplete information.
- Communicate technical facts as business risk.
- Recommend immediate and long-term actions to executives.
- Demonstrate an evidence-based GRC conclusion.

## Action items

1. Respond to four instructor injects.
2. Record decision, owner, deadline, rationale and evidence for each action.
3. Create a communication matrix for executives, regulators, law enforcement, employees and customers.
4. Write a one-page executive report using Situation, Impact, Root Cause, Actions and Decisions Needed.
5. Deliver a five-minute Risk Committee presentation.

## Questions to answer

- What should be done in the first four hours after suspicious activity is confirmed?
- When should business operations be interrupted to contain risk?
- What facts must be verified before public communication?
- What decisions require executive or Risk Committee approval?
- What is the single most important governance lesson?
