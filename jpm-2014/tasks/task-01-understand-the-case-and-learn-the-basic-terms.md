# Task 1: Understand the Case and Learn the Basic Terms

**Suggested time:** 60-75 minutes · **Primary lead:** GRC Coordinator

> **Required outputs**
>
> Case fact sheet; glossary check; 150-word breach summary; five questions for the instructor.

## Why this task matters

Students first separate verified facts from assumptions and learn the technical words needed for later tasks.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the breach in plain language.
- Identify the organization, affected people, exposed data, entry point and root cause.
- Distinguish a fact stated in the case from an assumption or unanswered question.

## Action items

1. Read the executive summary and incident timeline.
2. Review the beginner glossary before answering questions.
3. Complete the Who, What, When, Where, How and Impact fact sheet.
4. Write a 150-word summary that a non-technical manager could understand.
5. List five questions that would require additional evidence or investigation.

## Questions to answer

- Who was affected by the breach?
- What information was accessed?
- What information was not confirmed stolen?
- What system was used as the initial entry point?
- What required security control was missing from that system?
- Why is this described as a governance failure rather than only a technology failure?
- What is the difference between a verified fact and an assumption?
