# Task 2: Build the Incident Timeline and Identify Stakeholders

**Suggested time:** 75 minutes · **Primary lead:** Incident and Communications Analyst

> **Required outputs**
>
> Incident timeline; stakeholder map; responsibility and information-needs table.

## Why this task matters

A timeline shows how the incident developed. A stakeholder map shows who must act, approve, investigate or receive updates.

## Learning objectives

- Place key events in chronological order.
- Identify internal and external stakeholders.
- Explain what information each stakeholder needs and why.

## Action items

1. Create a timeline from reconnaissance through public disclosure and later prosecution.
2. Label each event as attack activity, detection, response, disclosure or legal follow-up.
3. List at least eight stakeholders.
4. For each stakeholder, identify their role, decision authority and information needs.
5. Identify two points where earlier action could have reduced the impact.

## Questions to answer

- When did the compromise begin, and when was it detected?
- How long were the attackers present before detection?
- What happened after suspicious activity was identified?
- Which stakeholders should have known about the unprotected server before the incident?
- Which stakeholders needed immediate notification after detection?
