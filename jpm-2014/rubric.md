# Final Grading Rubric

| **Assessment area** | **Weight** | **What good work looks like** |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding of case facts | 15% | Accurate facts; assumptions clearly labeled. |
| Risk identification and scoring | 20% | Clear cause-event-impact statements; defensible ratings. |
| Control and framework analysis | 15% | Relevant mappings supported by evidence. |
| Corrective actions and ownership | 20% | Specific, time-bound, measurable and assigned. |
| Monitoring and reporting | 10% | Useful KPIs/KRIs with thresholds and escalation. |
| Executive communication | 10% | Clear business language, concise recommendations and decisions needed. |
| Teamwork / individual contribution | 10% | Role completed, dependencies managed and quality review performed. |

> **Academic integrity**
>
> The instructor answer key contains model responses, not the only acceptable responses. Students may reach different conclusions when they explain their assumptions and support decisions with case evidence.
