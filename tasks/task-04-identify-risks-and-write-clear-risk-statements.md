# Task 4: Identify Risks and Write Clear Risk Statements

**Suggested time:** 90 minutes · **Primary lead:** Risk Analyst

> **Required outputs**
>
> Six cause-event-impact risk statements; control-gap table; risk owners.

## Why this task matters

A GRC professional must convert technical facts into business risks that management can understand and own.

## Learning objectives

- Use the cause-event-impact structure.
- Separate a control weakness from the business risk created by that weakness.
- Assign a logical risk owner.

## Action items

1. Identify at least six risks from the case.
2. Write each risk using: Because of [cause], there is a possibility that [event], resulting in [impact].
3. Identify the failed or missing control.
4. Assign a business risk owner and a supporting control owner.
5. State the affected confidentiality, integrity or availability objective.

## Questions to answer

- Is 'no MFA' a risk or a control gap?
- What business impacts could result from an incomplete asset inventory?
- Why should business owners participate in cybersecurity risk ownership?

> **Risk statement formula**
>
> Because of [cause/control weakness], there is a possibility that [risk event], resulting in [business impact].
