# Task 7: Design the Corrective Action and Exception Management Plan

**Suggested time:** 105 minutes · **Primary lead:** GRC Coordinator and Control Owners

> **Required outputs**
>
> 30-60-90 day corrective-action plan; exception register; RACI; residual-risk decisions.

## Why this task matters

A finding is not resolved until an owner completes an action, evidence is validated and remaining risk is formally accepted or closed.

## Learning objectives

- Select appropriate risk treatment actions.
- Create specific actions with owners and dates.
- Design a closed-loop exception process.
- Separate management implementation from independent validation.

## Action items

1. Choose mitigate, avoid, transfer or accept for each priority risk.
2. Create a 30-60-90 day plan.
3. Complete a sample exception register for the missing MFA control.
4. Build a RACI for asset registration, exception approval, remediation and validation.
5. Estimate residual risk after the plan and state who approves it.

## Questions to answer

- What makes a corrective action measurable?
- What information must every exception contain?
- Why should an externally accessible exception require higher approval?
- Who should validate closure?
